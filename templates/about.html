ABOUT THE PROJECT

Smart Hospital System

A digital approach to improving hospital emergency registration and queue management.

THE PROBLEM

Reducing unnecessary registration queues

Traditional hospital registration can involve long waiting lines, especially during busy emergency hours.

Smart Hospital provides a digital registration workflow where patients can receive a token and staff can manage the queue from a centralized dashboard.

01

Digital Registration

Patient details can be registered digitally.

02

Automatic Token

Every registration receives a unique token number.

03

Priority Queue

Emergency levels help organize the operational queue.

04

Live Dashboard

Staff can monitor and update patient status.
